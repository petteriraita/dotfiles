# Email Sending

Write short, direct email that sounds like Petteri rather than a template. Inspect the relevant thread or earlier correspondence when context, recipient, or tone matters.

## Style

- Lead with the request, decision, or update.
- Prefer two to five short paragraphs and plain language.
- Preserve Petteri's intended meaning and level of formality while cleaning up dictated fragments, repetition, and spelling.
- Remove generic pleasantries, puffery, repeated background, and stock phrases such as "I hope this email finds you well."
- Include only the context the recipient needs to understand or act.
- Do not invent facts, commitments, relationships, deadlines, or prior agreement.
- Do not claim that a document is attached unless it is actually attached.

## Draft and Send Boundary

- When asked to draft, prepare, write, or leave an email, create or update an unsent Gmail draft when Gmail is available.
- Send only when the user explicitly asks to send that specific message. Permission to draft is never permission to send.
- Before sending, verify the recipient, subject, final body, requested attachments, and whether the message belongs in an existing thread.
- When Gmail is unavailable, provide copy-ready text and say that it was not placed in Gmail.

## Expert Blind-Spot Check

For consequential outreach, negotiation, applications, or requests, end the user-facing response with a brief **What an expert would flag** section. Surface one to three material considerations the user did not ask about, including when the proposed framing, recipient, timing, or channel is strategically weak. Prefer concrete changes over generic cautions. Skip this section for routine wording edits or status confirmations when there is no meaningful additional issue.

## Academic Signatures

Use the full established signature matching the institution or identity Petteri represents in each academic email draft or reply, including brief replies. Do not replace it with a handwritten short sign-off such as “Best, Petteri,” and do not add both signatures. Follow a specific user request for a different sign-off when given.
