# Document Translation

Read the source, identify the target language and requested scope, and produce a clear, faithful translation in the requested format. Preserve names, dates, amounts, units, tables, qualifications, and material omissions; do not invent or silently repair information. Match the source structure when it helps the recipient compare the translation with the original. If translating an excerpt or summary, label its scope accurately.

Provide the translation itself without unsolicited commentary about AI authorship or whether it was independently verified. Do not label a translation certified, official, sworn, or verified unless that status is established. If a recipient requires certification or another specific form of translation, surface that requirement to the user rather than inventing credentials. Keep the original available and preserve its provenance.

For sensitive documents, use local sources and avoid sending their contents to unrelated services. Verify the produced file opens, is legible, and preserves key numbers and dates. Explain unresolved source ambiguities to the user without inventing facts.
